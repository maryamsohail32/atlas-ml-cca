ML Lab: Learning the Analog Score

The Lab 14 layer on the same ATLAS data. Machine learning models trained on the 56 base-site pairs (4 Moon bases × 14 Earth sites) learn to predict, classify and cluster the Analog Score from the five pillar distances alone.

Operator Guide
  • Base — switch from the top bar or the base cards; every panel re-ranks instantly.
  • Ranking — the orange ring on the map is the #1 match, mint is the runner-up; the dashed line is the top-3 path.
  • Finder — mission presets and weight sliders re-score all 14 sites live. Click any row for its discovery note.
  • Compare — radar shows per-factor similarity; a hollow marker means that factor had no data and was not scored.
  • Mission Log — ALERTS flag real data gaps, e.g. sites with no soil coverage.
  • Data — full CSV downloads live at the bottom of the Finder view.

Scores rank sites within this 14-site pool only. Full method, weights and limitations: Method view.
